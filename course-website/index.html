Chapter 1 · 01 / 04

Thyroid hormone (T3/T4)

The metabolic engine

The thyroid is your metabolic thermostat. If it's in "conservation mode", your body won't invest energy in building tissue, whatever your other hormones are doing. T4 is the storage form; T3 is the active form your cells need.

Growth hormone (HGH)

The conductor

HGH gives the orders; IGF-1 carries them out. It's released by the pituitary in short pulses, roughly 75% of it during deep sleep, so the goal is to make those pulses as high and as frequent as possible.

Chapter 3 · 03 / 04

IGF-1

The builder

IGF-1 is the actual executor of growth. Made mainly in the liver in response to HGH, and locally in muscle after mechanical load, it drives cell division, protein synthesis and the lengthwise growth of bone.

Liver, and locally in muscle
Key lever
